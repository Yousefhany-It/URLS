Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Shorts Poster ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your TikTok account, the App accesses only the permissions you explicitly authorize via TikTok's official OAuth process, such as the ability to upload and publish video content on your behalf.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. We do not access your TikTok account for any purpose beyond the specific automation you configure (e.g., publishing generated video clips).

3. Data Storage

Access tokens and credentials used to connect to TikTok are stored securely and used solely to perform the publishing actions you configure. Video content processed by the App is used only for the purpose of generating and publishing your clips.

4. Data Retention

Credentials and tokens are retained only as long as necessary to provide the service, or until you revoke access via your TikTok account settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The App interacts with TikTok's API to publish content. Your use of TikTok is also subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's account settings. You may also request deletion of any locally stored credentials by contacting the app owner.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app owner directly.
